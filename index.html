Traffic Simulator Guide

Indian Road Geometry & Rules

This simulator faithfully adheres to Left-Hand Drive (LHD) rules practiced across India:

  • Vehicles drive strictly on the left half of the roadway.
  • Free Left Turn: In Indian traffic systems, the left-most lane allows a continuous free left turn (subject to yield checks). You can toggle this on or off in the control panel.
  • Indian Vehicle Fleet: Spawns realistic proportions of Cars, Auto-Rickshaws, Two-Wheelers (motorcycles/scooters), Buses, and Emergency Ambulances.

100% Collision-Free Interlock Guarantee

The simulation employs a deterministic spatial reservation system. Vehicles entering the junction claim exclusive priority in their trajectory conflict cells. A 3-second all-red clearance interval guarantees no conflicting phase gets green while any vehicle is clearing the intersection.

Adaptive AI Mode vs Fixed-Time

In Fixed-Time Mode, green signals alternate on strict 20-second timers regardless of whether roads are empty. In Adaptive AI Mode, computer-vision queue sensors detect queue length and waiting delays, dynamically extending green time where needed and cutting empty phases short!

Emergency Green Corridor

Dispatches an ambulance with flashing emergency strobe lights and audio siren. The system safely completes amber clearance on active phases, opens a dedicated green corridor, and returns to normal operation smoothly once passed.
